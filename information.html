Privacy

How information is handled

This public website provides information about C3 Cyber Operations and links visitors to the C3 portal. If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to include. If you continue to the portal, information you provide there is used to create and operate your account, deliver assessment services, support your organisation and improve the service.

We only use personal information for the purpose for which it was provided, related service administration, security, support and legal obligations. We do not sell personal information. Service providers may process information on our behalf where needed to operate communications, hosting or the portal.

To request access, correction or deletion of personal information, or to raise a privacy concern, email hello@c3cyber.com.au. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

Website terms

Using this website

The content on this website is general information and is not legal, regulatory or professional advice. Illustrations, maturity scores and walkthrough examples demonstrate how the platform may operate; they are not an assessment of any particular organisation.

You may view and use this website for legitimate business information purposes. C3 Cyber Operations retains rights in the website content, branding and platform materials. External services and the C3 portal may have additional terms presented when you access them.

We work to keep the information accurate and available but cannot guarantee that every page will always be complete, current or uninterrupted. Contact us before relying on website content for a material decision.

Security

Security enquiries and responsible reporting

C3 Cyber Operations treats the confidentiality and integrity of organisational assessment information and supporting evidence as important. Access to the portal should only be provided to authorised users within your organisation, and account credentials should not be shared.

If you believe you have identified a security issue affecting this website or the C3 portal, please report it privately and include enough detail for us to investigate. Do not access, alter or download information that does not belong to you.

Send security reports to hello@c3cyber.com.au with the subject “Security report”.
