The whole operating picture

Six domains. One connected view.

The CSMM examines the management, human and technical capabilities that determine how well your organisation can anticipate, withstand and recover from cyber risk.

01

Governance & Risk Management

Strategy, accountability, risk decisions and oversight.

02

Protective Measures

Controls that reduce the likelihood and impact of compromise.

03

Detection Capability

Visibility and insight to identify suspicious activity early.

04

Response & Recovery

Preparedness to contain incidents and restore operations.

05

Compliance & Assurance

Evidence that obligations are understood and controls work.

06

Employee Training & Awareness

People equipped to recognise risk and act securely.
