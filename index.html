The whole operating picture

Six domains. One connected view.

The CSMM assesses six connected categories that show how an organisation governs cyber risk, protects its environment, identifies threats, responds to incidents, meets its obligations and prepares its people.

01

Governance and Risk Management

Evaluates policies, procedures and capabilities used to manage cybersecurity risk.

02

Protective Measures

Assesses physical and technical safeguards against cybersecurity threats.

03

Detection Capability

Measures the ability to identify cybersecurity events and potential breaches.

04

Response and Recovery

Examines processes for responding to and recovering from cybersecurity incidents.

05

Compliance and Assurance

Reviews adherence to legal, regulatory and policy requirements for cybersecurity.

06

Employee Training and Awareness

Gauges how effectively employees are educated about cybersecurity risks and responsibilities.

Why choose C3

Built to turn insight
into action.

C3 goes beyond producing another cyber score. It brings assessment, evidence, priorities and improvement work together so your organisation can make progress with confidence. Talk to us about your needs.

Why choose C3 instead of a one time cyber assessment?

A one time assessment can leave you with a score and a report. C3 connects your current position, target maturity, supporting evidence, priority gaps and improvement actions in one continuing workflow, so the assessment becomes the start of measurable progress, not the end of the exercise.

What makes C3 more practical than managing maturity in spreadsheets?

Spreadsheets separate answers from evidence, ownership and subsequent work. C3 keeps them connected in a secure organisational workspace, giving your team one place to assess capability, assign responsibility, retain evidence and track what happens next.

How does C3 help us decide what to improve first?

C3 shows the gap between where you are and the target that fits your organisation. Those gaps can be translated into a prioritised roadmap, helping leaders focus effort and investment on the improvements that matter most rather than treating every issue as equally urgent.

What do the five CSMM maturity levels mean?

1 · Ad hoc means the practice is mostly informal, reactive or unclear. 2 · Developing means important elements exist but are not yet consistent. 3 · Defined means the practice is documented, communicated and generally repeatable. 4 · Managed means it is measured, monitored and regularly reviewed. 5 · Optimised means evidence is used to improve it continuously. C3 also lets you choose an appropriate target for each area because reaching level five everywhere may not be necessary or the best use of investment.

Is C3 understandable for business leaders as well as cyber teams?

Yes. The platform turns detailed assessment work into clear maturity views, priority gaps and accountable actions. Leaders gain a credible view of direction and progress, while subject matter experts can work with the underlying responses and evidence.

How does C3 help a board understand cyber risk?

C3 translates detailed assessment work into a connected view of current maturity, target maturity, priority gaps and accountable actions. This helps directors focus on business exposure, investment choices and progress without needing to interpret every technical control.

Do board members need cybersecurity expertise to use C3 insights?

No. C3 gives the board a clear structure for oversight while allowing cyber and technology specialists to work with the detail underneath. Directors can concentrate on risk appetite, priorities, ownership and assurance.

How should a board use maturity targets?

A board can use target maturity to define the level of capability that is appropriate for the organisation and its risk context. The goal is not automatically to reach level five everywhere. It is to make deliberate choices about where stronger capability is needed and why.

What questions can directors ask after reviewing C3 results?

Directors can ask where the most important gaps are, which improvements should be funded first, who is accountable, what evidence supports the reported position and whether completed actions are producing measurable change.

Can the right people contribute without one person doing everything?

Yes. Bring governance, technology, risk, compliance and people specialists into the same process. Relevant modules can be assigned to the people closest to each area, while the organisation retains one connected view of responses, evidence and progress.

Does C3 support improvement beyond a single framework?

Yes. C3 combines the Cyber Security Maturity Model with readiness pathways for ASD Essentials, SOC 2 and NIST CSF. Relevant evidence, owners and actions can remain connected, reducing the fragmentation that comes from managing every readiness activity in a separate tool.

How does C3 help demonstrate real progress over time?

Improvement actions can be assigned, tracked and supported by updated evidence. As work is completed and assessment responses are revisited, C3 helps your organisation show how capability is changing rather than simply showing that an assessment was completed.

Can we experience the platform before committing?

Yes. New organisations receive a 14 day trial with no credit card required, giving you time to explore the platform and begin establishing your baseline. If more time is genuinely needed, C3 Cyber Operations can review a trial extension before you decide on annual access.
